### About me

I'm a **Software Engineering** student and co-founder of **ecodex**, where I lead **strategy and technology**: I decide how each business problem becomes a solution and make sure what we ship actually works.

I come from development (mobile apps with **Flutter and Dart**) and chose **software quality** as my specialty. That means I look at every delivery with a tester's eye: APIs, UI, data flow, and the edge cases that break in production. Today I build automated test suites with **Python, pytest, Selenium, and Requests**, and I'm growing toward **SDET and CI/CD**.

### Recent work at ecodex

We're building the ecodex website from scratch, with a diagnostic intake form and a private leads dashboard.

- **Leads admin API:** search, filters, and editing with optimistic locking (conflicts return `409`, so nobody silently overwrites someone else's work).
- **Dashboard metrics API:** funnel numbers checked one by one against raw SQL queries on the database.
- **Accessibility, performance, and SEO QA (with Ryan):** zero axe violations across 5 screens and 4 viewport widths; mobile LCP down from 2.7 s to under 2 s.
- **Code review and testing:** reviewed the intake form deliveries (multi-step and one-question-at-a-time dialog); the project has **900+ automated tests**.
- **Visual direction and architecture:** defined the site's visual direction and stack with Ryan (TypeScript, React, Cloudflare Workers, and D1).
- **Field diagnosis:** mapped local businesses and ran mystery shopper simulations to find where operations lose time and money.
